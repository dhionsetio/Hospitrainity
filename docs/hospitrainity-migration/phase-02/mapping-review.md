# Phase 2 — Alignment Review Report

## Result
The machine-readable competency framework, CEFR reference register and 21 local outcomes are complete as **drafts pending qualified review**. Seven modules each contain three outcomes.

## Safeguards
- Local outcomes are never presented as verbatim CEFR descriptors.
- Verbatim CEFR text is isolated in `cefr-references.json` with scale, level and official source URI.
- Every outcome has observable evidence conditions and competency links.
- Reflection in Module 1 is local/non-graded and has no invented CEFR alignment.
- The framework uses a profile across A2–B1 rather than asserting a single global level.

## Review gate
A qualified ESP/CEFR reviewer must verify each descriptor transcription, level, scale and alignment rationale. A hospitality practitioner must validate workplace authenticity. Until both reviews are recorded, status remains `draft_pending_qualified_review`; no learner-facing CEFR claim is permitted.

## Research note
The official Council of Europe PDF and searchable descriptor registry are the authority. The sandbox could not directly download the PDF because outbound DNS is disabled; descriptor text was cross-checked through indexed Council of Europe results and a complete-scale reference copy. This limitation is explicitly retained for human review rather than hidden.
