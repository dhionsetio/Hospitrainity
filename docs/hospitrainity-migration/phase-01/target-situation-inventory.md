# Phase 1 — Target-Situation Inventory

Bounded by `Hospitrainity.docx`; this adds no learning material.

| Module | Situation | Channel | Observable performance | Complexity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer-care orientation | Reflection/awareness | Identify warmth, attentiveness, problem-solving, positive language, confidence | Non-graded |
| 2 | Front desk/check-in | Face-to-face | Greet, confirm, request documents, explain key information, offer help, close | Routine but accuracy-sensitive |
| 3 | Telephone | Telecommunications | Open, gather, clarify/spell/read back, hold/transfer, close | No visual cues |
| 4 | Guest-services hotline | Phone + handoff | Identify need/room, take request/order, estimate, transfer, confirm, follow up | Accuracy and coordination |
| 5 | Writing | Email/letter | Structure reply, confirm, choose register, respond/apologise, close | Tone/completeness |
| 6 | Online/social | Chat/public/DM | Reply briefly, protect privacy, escalate, maintain tone | Public/time pressure |
| 7 | Complaints | Spoken + written | Listen, empathize, apologise, own, act, follow up | Highest/emotional |

## Phase 2 competency candidates
Task achievement; information accuracy; interaction/clarification; politeness; positive-language problem solving; intelligibility; written clarity; channel register; self-regulation; department handoff/mediation.

## Excluded legacy scope
Legacy grammar courses, broad pronunciation units, emergencies, local tours, and other topics are excluded unless Phase 4 proves DOCX chapter fit and removes unsupported details.
