# Phase 1 — Provisional CEFR Level Strategy

## Status
**Working hypothesis only; not approved for learner-facing claims.** No evidence establishes the intended cohort's entry-level distribution.

## A2–B1 planning hypothesis
Predictable routine exchanges may align with A2 descriptors. Sustained complaints, professional correspondence, telephone clarification, and goal-oriented online interaction may require B1 performance. Different modes may sit at different levels, so a profile is preferable to one global label.

## Phase 2 approval method
1. Build the official 2020 Companion Volume descriptor register.
2. Write local hospitality can-do outcomes.
3. Map each outcome to exact text, scale, level, location, and rationale.
4. Separate reception, production, interaction, mediation, online interaction, and competence scales.
5. Obtain ESP/CEFR review and compare with learner diagnostic evidence.

## Prohibited claims
Completing Hospitrainity does not prove B1, CEFR certification, workplace readiness, or proficiency from confidence/quiz scores alone.
