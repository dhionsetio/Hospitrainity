# Phase 1 — Needs-Analysis Brief

**Status:** checkpoint-complete. **Scope:** analysis only; no content import, runtime change, or rebrand yet.

## Primary learners
Pre-service D4 English for Business and Professional Communication (BIKBP) and Tourism students at Politeknik Negeri Malang preparing for hotel guest-facing work.

## Exclusions
In-service hotel staff, general public, high-stakes certification, and claims about actual workplace service quality or guest satisfaction.

## Known characteristics
- Authentic practice is needed before work placement.
- Some learners lack laptops; mobile access is mandatory.
- Excessive difficulty/theory can reduce engagement.
- Practice, role-play, simulation, simple explanations, relevance, and immediate feedback are preferred.

## Unknowns
Actual CEFR entry distribution, cohort profile, prior hospitality experience, accessibility requirements, network/data constraints, browser range, and supervised/self-study balance. These remain evidence gaps—not assumptions.

## Target needs
The seven DOCX chapters define the complete task boundary: customer-care orientation; front desk/check-in; telephone service; guest-services hotline; written service; online/social service; and complaints.

Learners must speak/write clearly, maintain hospitality tone, confirm information, clarify, handle requests/orders/messages, coordinate handoffs, use positive language, and resolve complaints through listening, apology, ownership, action, and follow-up. The expert interview identifies complaints as the hardest situation; routine check-in/out is more procedure-driven.

## Learning needs
- Scenario-first, action-oriented tasks.
- Model → guided imitation → role-play/simulation → immediate feedback → retry/reflection.
- Calibrated difficulty and concise mobile-friendly steps.
- Confidence stored separately from achievement.
- English-only content and seven equal top-level modules.

## Confirmed constraints
DOCX-only authority; Grand Serenata Hotel only; chapter-aligned legacy rewriting; unsupported details removed; unmatched content removed; old progress archived; new progress begins at 0%.

## Conclusion
Proceed to Phase 2, but do not publish a final CEFR label. A mixed A2–B1 pathway is a planning hypothesis only: predictable exchanges may align near A2, while complaints, correspondence, telephone clarification, and online service may require B1 performance. Phase 2 must map official descriptors and obtain qualified review.
