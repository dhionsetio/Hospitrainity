# Phase 1 — Evidence-Bounded Learner Personas

Personas guide design; they are not claims about every learner.

## A. Mobile-first pre-service learner
D4 BIKBP/Tourism student; not yet in-service; may lack a laptop; needs short scenarios, mobile controls, repetition, and immediate feedback. Unknowns: CEFR level, accessibility, network conditions.

## B. Procedure-ready but complaint-anxious learner
Can learn routine SOP-like exchanges but has limited experience with unpredictable emotional interactions. Needs modeled stages, guided choices, role-play, self-regulation cues, positive language, and follow-up practice.

## C. Writing/online-service learner
Needs to handle enquiries, orders, complaints, email, live chat, and public-to-private escalation. Needs models, register comparison, content checklists, error-specific feedback, and revision.

## Consequences
No desktop-only or theory-first workflow. Do not equate confidence with competence, assume one entry level, or generate Indonesian curriculum content.
